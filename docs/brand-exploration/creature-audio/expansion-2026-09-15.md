Prioritize short, individual creature performances: curious little questions, laughter, happy reactions, soft coos, nasal babble and rounded alien trills. Background music, unrelated noise, watermarks and surrounding demonstration sounds are exclusion criteria. A model describing a sound as clean is screening evidence, not human listening approval.

These investigations favor individual files over mixed promotional reels. Every included clip carries its source and usage statement in the listening room.

## Screening decisions

The first combined montage retained nine vocal candidates and withheld five: one mouth-pop and four slime sounds that read as toy/suction noises. The creator’s description of the slime source also identifies a paper-wobble ingredient. A further two montages screened Little Robot calls; 19 expressive voices survived, with growls and mouth-click/suction sounds withheld. The retained set has no music or unrelated noise flagged by the source/montage screening; user listening remains decisive. This distinction matters because an isolated sound file can still be the wrong kind of sound.
