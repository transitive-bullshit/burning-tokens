# Isolated creator voice candidates

**Research and cut notes · 15 September 2026.** Three creator clips are included in the listening room: two melodic phrases from szegvari and one squeaky “nyam-nyam” voice from Hawkeye_Sprout. Four staged SilverIllusionist slime clips were withheld after the final combined audition audit. No previously reviewed or starred sound is replaced.

## Prepared sources

- [Cute Creature Sing — szegvari](https://freesound.org/people/szegvari/sounds/546761/) credits voice actor **Mr ROTPB**, describes a recorded and processed vocal, and releases the sound as [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Two different phrases from the first half are retained; the repeated second half is omitted. Automated source analysis describes a high, cheerful vocal melody without instrumental accompaniment. This is a singing voice, rather than a background music bed.
- [Slime Creature Movement (small and cute) — SilverIllusionist](https://freesound.org/people/SilverIllusionist/sounds/671111/) is [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The creator requests the credit **“Slime Creature Movement (small and cute)” by SilverIllusionist** and credits **Wobble paper.wav by deleted_user_2104797** and **Squeaking Guinea Pigs by Breviceps** as ingredients. Four separated calls were staged but are **withheld from the listening room**. The final combined audition audit perceived wet popping, suction-cup or toy foley rather than clear voices. That finding is consistent with the creator’s composite ingredients.
- [Cute creature 01.wav — Hawkeye_Sprout](https://freesound.org/people/Hawkeye_Sprout/sounds/469066/) is [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). One high-pitched “nyam-nyam” phrase is retained. This is a performed, word-like creature voice. Credit the creator and original title.

The publicly playable high-quality MP3 previews were retrieved from the media URLs exposed by the creator pages. Original WAV download buttons require a Freesound login, which was not used. These preview encodings are suitable for listening comparisons; the source pages remain the production acquisition and attribution references.

All cuts retain source pitch and speed. Each has a 12 ms entrance and 80 ms exit fade, is peak-matched to −6 dBFS before encoding, and is exported as mono 48 kHz / 128 kbps MP3. Decoded peaks are −6.47 to −6.34 dBFS. All seven files decode successfully and contain finite non-silent samples.

Cut boundaries use 10 ms RMS analysis. The singing phrases are separated by a quiet valley; their 10 ms overlap contains only the low-level valley and tail, not duplicate voiced content. The slime calls have large quiet gaps. The Hawkeye recording starts during the vocal attack, so the opening is softened rather than falsely described as a silent boundary. Final 30 ms RMS levels are below −57 dBFS in every exported file.

## Selection limits

The final combined montage audit retained `szegvari-moon-song`, `szegvari-singing-answer` and `hawkeye-nyam`. It rejected all four `silver-slime-*` candidates as wet popping or toy-like foley. Their working files remain available for research, but they are excluded from the listening-room integration.

A bounded automated analysis described each source. It is useful for choosing audition material, but it does not establish music-free quality. The slime response supplied an impossible timestamp beyond the actual 5.719-second file; its timestamps were discarded and the physical waveform determined the cut windows. The Hawkeye response rejected a human-performed “nyam-nyam” sound despite that being a valid direction for the user's creature voices; its descriptive observation was retained, not its category judgment. Human listening remains decisive.

[Baby Tesla Dragon Cry](https://freesound.org/people/deleted_user_3424813/sounds/232106/) was retrieved but withheld after an unhelpful automated rejection. [Cute Anime Giggle](https://freesound.org/people/DarkNightPrincess/sounds/611780/) and [Video Game Squeak](https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/468442/) were also withheld: the former leans toward a recognizable human voice, while the latter lasts just 0.16 seconds and is closer to an electronic squeak than a character reaction. None of these withheld sounds is in the addition fragment.
