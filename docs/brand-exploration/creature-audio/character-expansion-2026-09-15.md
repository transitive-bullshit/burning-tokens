# Character voices: a different sourcing pass

**15 September 2026 · [Audition the new candidates](./?collection=new)**

The previous 28-clip expansion produced three user favorites: `tiny-creature-laugh`, `ambroggio-me`, and `hawkeye-nyam`. All 19 Little Robot Sound Factory candidates were hidden. Earlier Kyotosomo and Minion-reference clips had much stronger acceptance. This pass therefore follows expressive, performed character voices and the specific creator behind a new favorite, instead of expanding the generic alien libraries.

## What changed

The 26 new candidates span four sourced families and one original experiment. Their placement alternates creators, so the opening audition covers different voice textures. All 21 prior favorites remain starred, all 41 rejections remain hidden, and all earlier audio files and clip IDs are untouched. The cumulative review is backed up separately from the new batch.

The Hawkeye source files include explicitly creature-like calls and more human-adjacent laughter, sighs, humming and yawns. Those are an intentional comparison within a performer the user already liked, not an assertion that all will fit. The dataset’s raw pool contains excerpts of originals; clip timestamps refer to those supplied files, with original Freesound IDs and dataset paths retained.

## Withheld directions

- BABAKA’s [Slime Voice Pack](https://babaka-audio.itch.io/slime-voice-pack) has 220 sounds and costs $9 at research time. Its public demo has a continuous audio bed, with no quiet interval below −40 dBFS until the end. That does not identify the bed by itself; the excerpts were withheld because vocal isolation was uncertain.
- Squeaky Creatures and the Audios With Love cartoon demo were withheld after existing audio checks reported music or narration. Shannon Scott’s creature reel leaned toward aggressive performances. The remaining fvcalderan battle cries were also withheld.
- The original synthesis experiment generated four six-second takes. Three missed the requested character, producing strained, crying or otherwise unsuitable sounds. Only an isolated opening giggle was retained. More generation was not used to pad the count.

Professional catalogs are audition leads, with a route to licensing a full vocabulary if their voice character fits. No library was purchased, no account created, and no creator contacted. The existing [Fun Monsters library](https://articulatedsounds.com/audio-royalty-free-library/sfx/fun-monsters) remains another route to expanding its already-liked Cuty/Kitty direction.

## Checks and limits

All 26 new MP3s are 0.27–1.73 seconds long, decode successfully, and have measured levels and checksums. Both longer professional demos have real quiet gaps below −43 dBFS; Furble calls are individually supplied audition files. Trims and fades follow those boundaries. These checks do not constitute perceptual certification that every voice is music-free or suitable; the user’s listening review remains decisive.

Automatic approval review blocked uploading a third-party demo excerpt to fal for further analysis because that external transfer was outside the approved scope. That action was stopped. After the working agents were informed, no further third-party uploads or analysis jobs were submitted; remaining checks used local files and primary source information. Analysis jobs already submitted before the rejection were allowed to finish. The synthesis experiment used only original text prompts and its own generated outputs.
