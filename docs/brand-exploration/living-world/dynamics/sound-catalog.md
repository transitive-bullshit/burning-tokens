## Reaction roles

Curious questions and short greetings favor hover and pickup. Surprised breaths, rising ooh-like reactions and excited squeals favor quick movement while held. Laughter and delighted reactions favor a gentle landing or nudge. Soft hums, affectionate sighs, small babble, gentle feline calls and yawns can supply sparse idle life. A yawn is not labeled as a surprised reaction just to fill a coverage gap.

Room counts overlap because a dish, page or chime can fit more than one space. Targets identify material or object relationships: `water`, `fire`, `kettle`, `ceramic`, `paper`, `fabric`, `wood`, `chime`, `bell`, `electric`, `switch`, `magic`, `footstep`, `soft`, `metal` and `tool`. An effect should match both the room and the relevant target rather than play any approved effect from that room.

The completed pass leaves 17 effects eligible for occasional idle playback: one bubble and two wave washes, two fire excerpts, one kettle excerpt, three fabric cues, two page turns, one pencil mark, one padded footstep and four individual gentle tones. Those four tones are `room-vcsl-hand-chimes-2`, `room-vcsl-bell-tree-individual-1`, `room-vcsl-vibraphone-soft-mallets-1` and `room-vcsl-glockenspiel-1`. They are isolated instrument responses, not music beds. No gong, magic phrase, surprise, splash or electrical activation is classified as an automatic idle effect. The sound director spaces background cues 12–25 seconds apart at low gain and suppresses them while hovering or interacting. Long excerpts remain finite and should fade or be interrupted for a foreground interaction; they are not loops or a persistent soundtrack.

The 19 active voice clips labeled `idle` describe future suitable creature chatter; the current random scheduler plays room effects only. Their other interaction roles and family fallbacks remain available now.

## Asset and provenance contract

- `src` is relative to the original creature-audio library. The build resolves it against `../../creature-audio/`; no media is re-encoded or renamed.
- Every clip keeps its original stable `id`, duration and source URL. Join that ID to [clips.json](../../creature-audio/clips.json) for full creator, license, source timestamp, processing and checksum records. Source permissions remain unchanged; starred preference is not production clearance.
- `families` defines voice identity. A creature should retain its assigned family across hover, pickup, quick movement, drop, room switches and section changes.
- `roles`, `rooms` and `targets` are explicit routing metadata. Voices have no room restriction. Room effects have at least one material or object target and never impersonate a creature voice.
- `effectType: texture` marks a bounded source excerpt. This pass does not add music, looping ambience or a continuously playing background track.
