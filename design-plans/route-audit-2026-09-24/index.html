Seven changes to review

Audited at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px. Populated gallery, visitor and administrator views use browser-only sample responses. Decisions below are saved in this browser; they do not change code. Export them when you are ready for the subsequent accept/reject pass.

UI-01 · P2 · 1440 px desktop

Use the approved editorial typeface everywhere

font-serif resolved to the browser serif instead of bundled Fraunces. The shared serif token now resolves to Fraunces, with the documented display axes and heading weight.

Affects: Send, About subheadings, gallery, public/private visits, administrator pages

Implementation: app/globals.css · Full before capture · Full after capture

UI-02 · P2 · 1440 px desktop

Give About one centered reading column

Unlayered .page-intro CSS overrode max-w-3xl and py-16, leaving a 1600px article with left-aligned text and centered CTAs. A dedicated about-page owner restores a 48rem column and consistent vertical rhythm.

Affects: /about

Implementation: app/about/page.tsx + app/globals.css · Full before capture · Full after capture

UI-03 · P2 · 320 px narrow mobile

Make small-screen navigation readable

At 320px the two navigation labels broke mid-phrase, while the logo shrank below the documented 180px minimum. The narrow header now gives navigation its own row; active routes have an underline and aria-current.

Affects: Shared header on every route

Implementation: app/layout.tsx + app/globals.css · Full before capture · Full after capture

UI-04 · P2 · 390 px mobile

Give mobile controls room to tap and read

Mobile buttons were 32–36px high; courtyard arrows were about 23px, and the sound toolbar squeezed its hint into a narrow right column. Key controls now have 44px targets, room labels are 14px, and the scene hint takes its own row.

Affects: Shared buttons/toggles, header/footer links, room tabs, scene toolbar and courtyard navigation

Implementation: app/globals.css + app/world.css · Full before capture · Full after capture

UI-05 · P1 · 390 px mobile · browser sample visit

Keep journey states visually truthful

Native scene button rules overrode shadcn switches, selected filters and destructive actions. Native selectors now exclude data-slot primitives. Destructive buttons use ink on coral (6.65:1), and switches transition only their changing properties with a reduced-motion alternative.

Affects: Private journey panel; shared destructive button and switch

Implementation: app/world.css + components/ui/button.tsx + components/ui/switch.tsx · Full before capture · Full after capture

UI-06 · P2 · 390 px mobile

Keep creatures clear of the invitation labels

Percentage-based companion positions overlapped the stacked mobile actions. In narrow containers, all untouched creatures now perch above the primary button; user-dragged positions are preserved.

Affects: Homepage and About CTA compositions

Implementation: components/retreat/cta-creatures.tsx · Full before capture · Full after capture

UI-07 · P2 · 390 px mobile · unavailable visit

Resolve the unavailable-visit dead end

The error message said the visit was unavailable while the page still promised “Opening your private journal…”. The contradictory loading line is replaced with a route back to invitations; actual loading retains its status announcement.

Affects: /visit/:id when no visit snapshot is available

Implementation: components/retreat/watch-visit.tsx · Full before capture · Full after capture
